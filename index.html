The Atelier Protocol

Precision Tailoring in Four Clear Steps

Commissioning custom cushions or yacht upholstery is direct and collaborative. Nasrin oversees every measure, pattern, and stitch.

01 Photo & Dimensions

Snap & Send Dimensions

Take a quick photo of your window bay, bench, boat cabin, or sagged cushion. Text rough measurements (length × width × depth) directly to Nasrin.

02 Material Selection

Fabric & Core Curation

Select from our curated Belgian linens, marine vinyls, or performance velvets — or supply your own fabric. We specify the ideal foam density.

03 Atelier Benchwork

Templating & Hand Stitching

Physical pattern templating for bespoke angles. Fabric is hand-sheared, piped with matching or contrast cord, and reinforced with bonded industrial thread.

04 Precision Fitting

Fitting & Perfect Fit Guarantee

Collection from our Northern Beaches studio or direct on-site installation across Sydney's Northern Beaches & Harbour waterways.

Foam Formulations
Enduro & Reticulated

From ultra-soft feather wrapped cores to extra-firm 42/300 commercial density and marine QuickDry.

Seam Engineering
Double French & Corded Piping

Industrial YKK zips concealed within reverse flange seams for effortless laundering and long-term durability.
