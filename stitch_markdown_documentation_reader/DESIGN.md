## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the tactile elegance of high-end bespoke craftsmanship. Rooted in artisanal textiles, tailoring traditions, and residential upholstery, the aesthetic is warm, unhurried, and authentic. It deliberately rejects sterile SaaS tropes, synthetic tech gradients, and generic corporate layouts in favor of an editorial magazine structure reminiscent of bespoke interior architecture publications.

The emotional signature is quiet luxury, mastery, and bespoke precision. The interface evokes natural unbleached fibres, chalk marks, cut shears, and tailored seams. Visual hierarchy relies on generous open space, crisp contrast between charcoal typography and natural woven stone, hairline border dividers, and high-impact photography of finished works. Emojis and artificial embellishments are completely prohibited; iconography must strictly adhere to restrained, hairline 1.5px monochrome vector lines.

## Colors

The color palette draws directly from tactile atelier raw materials:
- **Canvas Base (`#FAF8F5`):** The ground layer of the interface, referencing raw unbleached Belgian linen. It eliminates eye fatigue associated with stark digital white and introduces warmth.
- **Surface Card (`#F2EEE9`):** Chalked limestone surface for secondary tiers, image mount backings, form inputs, and subtle container distinctions.
- **Tailor Charcoal (`#1A1918`):** Deep, organic off-black derived from tailor's marking chalk. Provides editorial authority for primary titles, structural rules, and primary text.
- **Muted Stone (`#635E59`):** Neutral body tone for descriptions, technical dimensions, materials breakdown, and quiet metadata.
- **Seam Border Line (`#E3DDD5`):** The structural gridline color representing structural baselines and tailoring cut-lines.
- **Terracotta Craft Dye (`#8C4E37`):** Earthy accent tone used for micro-details, subtle interactive hovers, and textile tags.
- **Marine Canvas Navy (`#2C3947`):** Deep oceanic navy referencing bespoke nautical upholstery, heavy canvas weaves, and telephone consultation anchors.

Synthetic gradient ramps, vibrant neon hues, and ungrounded saturated primary fills are strictly avoided.

## Typography

The typographic balance pairs an authoritative, high-contrast transitional serif (`Playfair Display`) with an airy, contemporary humanist geometric sans (`Plus Jakarta Sans`).

- **Headlines & Display:** `Playfair Display` provides the refined air of heritage atelier ledgers and luxury architectural journals. Display scales use tight negative tracking on desktop to heighten cohesion, relaxing slightly on smaller breakpoints.
- **Editorial Body:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` is configured with extended line heights (`1.7` to `1.85`) to mimic high-end long-form publishing. This spacing ensures technical upholstery specifications, fabric grades, and personal artisan stories read with clarity.
- **Micro-Labels & Metadata:** All categories, process steps, and specimen tags use small uppercase tracking (`0.15em`) in medium-to-bold weights to act as structured annotations.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is structured around an asymmetrical editorial grid that pairs wide image canvases with precise typography columns.
- **Grid Philosophy:** A 12-column adaptive fluid grid with rigid 1px structural framing lines. Rather than centering everything identically, content blocks interlock in a staggered, magazine-like cadence (e.g., 7-column feature photograph next to a 5-column offset detail panel).
- **Rhythm & Scale:** Vertical pacing is deliberate and expansive. Major portfolio pieces and atelier philosophy sections are demarcated by generous breaks (`space-3xl` / `6rem`), avoiding the cramped density common in commercial landing pages.
- **Responsive Adaptations:**
  - **Desktop (1024px+):** Full 12-column grid with alternating asymmetrical visual mounts, wide side margins (`margin-desktop`), and prominent vertical boundary lines.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 6-column grid with proportional reductions in outer canvas gutters. Side-by-side elements fold into stacked pairs.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column presentation with `1.25rem` outer margins. Edge-to-edge imagery with inset hairline frames allows craft details to dominate narrow viewports.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids floating UI layers and artificial drop shadows. Depth is conveyed strictly through real-world material stacking and hairline structural boundaries:
- **Tonal Layering:** The primary background rests on unbleached linen (`#FAF8F5`). Embedded works, cards, and inspection areas sit on chalked limestone (`#F2EEE9`), establishing a natural, physical step without drop shadows.
- **Hairline Seam Dividers:** 1px solid outlines in `#E3DDD5` delineate sections, image frames, and structural cells, evoking hand-stitched borders and tailored seams.
- **Active / Pressed Tactility:** Rather than elevating toward the user with shadows upon hover or interaction, elements react through internal tone shifts (e.g., `#F2EEE9` shifting to `#FAF8F5`), crisp hairline color switches (`#E3DDD5` to `#8C4E37`), or micro-transforms. Floating cards with diffused shadows are forbidden.

## Shapes

The shape system is strictly architectural and precise:
- **Border Radius:** Global radius is constrained to an absolute maximum of 4px (`rounded-sm` / level `1`), with several critical structural blocks remaining fully crisp at 0px (`rounded-none`).
- **Pill Badges Forbidden:** Pill shapes (`rounded-full`), circular capsules, or bulbous container buttons are strictly disallowed as they contradict the bespoke tailoring aesthetic.
- **Aspect Ratios:** Photography mounts utilize classic editorial framing (1:1 square, 4:3 workshop view, 3:4 tailored vertical portrait) bound within crisp 1px borders.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Action (Direct Call & Consultation):** Background in marine canvas navy (`#2C3947`), text in `#FAF8F5`, 4px corner radius, padding `1rem 2rem`. Hover transitions smoothly to tailor charcoal (`#1A1918`).
- **Secondary Action (Inquiry & Fabric Request):** Transparent background, 1px border in `#1A1918`, text in `#1A1918`. Hover fills subtly with `#F2EEE9` or transitions border to craft terracotta (`#8C4E37`).
- **Icon Buttons:** Strictly 1.5px hairline stroke SVGs housed in square or 4px rounded frames. No solid colored circular floats.

### Cards & Project Showcases
- Flat surfaces filled with `#F2EEE9` bordered by a 1px `#E3DDD5` outline.
- Padding inside cards adheres to `space-lg` (1.5rem) or `space-xl` (2.5rem).
- Image headers within cards fit flush to the border or rest centered on an intentional chalk mat with generous spacing.

### Form Inputs & Textareas
- Background uses `#FAF8F5` or `#FFFFFF` enclosed by a 1px `#E3DDD5` perimeter.
- Focus state switches the border cleanly to `#8C4E37` with zero glowing halos or drop-shadow rings.
- Placeholder text in `#635E59` with subtle typography; labels set in `label-uppercase`.

### Chips, Badges & Fabric Tags
- Minimalist rectangular tags with 2px to 4px corners.
- Fill `#F2EEE9`, border 1px `#E3DDD5`, text set in `label-uppercase` using `#1A1918` or `#635E59`.
- No rounded-full capsules.

### Lists & Technical Specifications
- Dividers between specification line items (e.g., Fabric Composition, Foam Density, Piping Finish) rendered with 1px `#E3DDD5` rules.
- Key-value pairs arranged with left-aligned muted labels and right-aligned charcoal editorial text.
